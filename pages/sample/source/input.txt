Make a quiet two-page comic called Rooftop Stories. After a busy day, Mena climbs to an
apartment rooftop. She sits safely by the parapet and looks across the city. A pigeon
settles beside her. The city keeps moving, but Mena finds a little room to breathe.
Use four panels, contemporary pen-and-ink city art, and short first-person captions.
Keep Mena's curly black hair, red hoodie, black trousers, and off-white sneakers consistent.
