The making of a page

From one sentence
to a bound book.

The engine moves a project through a fixed lifecycle. Studio shows you where you are, what the engine needs next, and refuses nothing silently: when a step is not ready, it tells you why.

  1. INIT

    Begin

    A short prompt, a pasted story up to 200 KB, one of the engine's starter comics, or a portable archive from another machine.

  2. PLANNED · SCRIPTED · STORYBOARDED

    Plan

    Story beats, two to five scenes, a cast with visual fingerprints, and one to four pages of panels. Draft it with Claude or GPT, or write it yourself in the plan editor.

  3. REFERENCES_READY · PANELS_READY

    Render

    Reference sheets you approve, then panels at the exact pixel size of their frame.

  4. QA_READY

    Review

    Every panel is judged on seven checks before it can be lettered, and every character on seven traits. Evidence has to name what is actually in the image.

    • character identity
    • anatomy
    • action
    • composition
    • continuity
    • text-free art
    • technical

  5. LETTERED · COMPOSED

    Letter and compose

    Balloons, captions, and lettered effects drawn by the engine in Comic Neue, then pages assembled and checked for clipped text and crowding.

  6. EXPORTED · COMPLETE

    Bind

    A PDF whose every page is decoded and verified, a QA report, and an archive that opens in any Comic Sol install.

The engine underneath

Exact where it matters.

One identity, every panel.

The engine derives an identity pack from your cast and writes the same identity lock into every panel prompt, with the approved reference sheets attached in a fixed order. A retry asks for the same character, not a paraphrase of one.

Lettering is never generated.

Image models are told to leave room and draw no words. The engine sets every balloon itself and refuses text that does not fit its panel.

Revisions you can trust.

Every write is tied to the version you were looking at. If an agent or another window changed the project, Studio stops and shows you the newer one.

Run it

Your machine,
your keys.

Studio listens on 127.0.0.1 only. Projects are folders you own. Model keys stay in the environment you start Studio from and never reach the page. Without any key, every step still works: upload your own art and review it yourself.

Start Studio

pip install -e .
comicsol-studio --data-root /absolute/path/to/studio-data

Optional model routes

ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=…   plan drafts and visual review with Claude
OPENAI_API_KEY=…      plan drafts, review, and panel rendering
