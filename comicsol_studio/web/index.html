The workflow

A page takes
more than a prompt.

You decide what goes into the comic. Studio keeps the story, artwork, and reviews together, and shows what needs attention before the next step.

  1. Begin

    Start with an idea, paste a story, choose a starter comic, or import an archive.

  2. Plan

    Write the scenes and cast, then lay out one to four pages. Draft with a connected model or work directly in the editor.

  3. Render

    Approve character references. Upload your artwork or render panels to fit their frames.

  4. Review

    Check each panel against the plan. Accept it, leave a warning, or send it back for another attempt.

    • character identity
    • anatomy
    • action
    • composition
    • continuity
    • text-free art
    • technical

  5. Letter and compose

    Comic Sol places balloons, captions, and sound effects. Read the assembled pages and check the lettering.

  6. Export

    Download the checked PDF and review report. Save an archive to carry the project to another Comic Sol install.

Page layouts

Give each scene
the space it needs.

Keep the cast consistent.

Your character descriptions and approved references accompany each panel prompt. When you retry a panel, Comic Sol reuses those same details.

Set the lettering separately.

Panel prompts ask for text-free art. Comic Sol adds the words from your script and flags text that does not fit.

Keep edits in sync.

If another window or tool changes a project, Studio loads the newer version before you can overwrite it.

Local setup

Keep the studio
on your desk.

Install Studio from its source repository and open it in your browser. Projects stay in your chosen data folder. Connected models receive the material needed for their task.
