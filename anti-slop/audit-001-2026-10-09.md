## Reasons for major decisions

- Color: retain the owner's obsidian and amber; artwork remains the focal point.
- Type: one locally bundled sans-serif follows the owner's modern-font request without adding a dependency.
- Layout: rows suit ordered production stages; forms and artwork use the space their content needs.
- Spacing: reduce repeated panel padding and mobile whitespace so the next action remains reachable.
- Containers: borders identify controls and warnings; ordinary editor sections need only a dividing rule.
- Images: retain the browser-drawn book, explicitly labelled a specimen, and real engine output in the replay.
- Icons: keep task and navigation symbols; remove decoration unrelated to an action.
- Motion: retain the opening-book demonstration; editing screens only animate state changes.

## Limits

Paid provider calls were tested with existing mocked transports, not real keys. Browser checks used
Chromium viewport emulation, not physical phones or a screen reader. Reduced-motion and WebGL
fallback paths were inspected in source; no forced GPU failure was simulated. The branch is prepared
for review, not merged or deployed. Visual taste remains subjective; the report does not claim an
objective guarantee that nobody will perceive an AI influence.
