## Design reasons

- Warm paper puts the ink drawings first; red links the cover, clothing and principal actions.
- Manrope stays locally bundled and answers the owner's simple, modern type request.
  The generated cover uses a condensed title; engine captions use Comic Neue.
- Script plus panel, then page plus editing explanation, give each section the proportions
  its content needs. Layout diagrams use the actual engine rectangles.
- Rules separate content without repeated cards. Small control corners and restrained paper
  shadows follow the print direction.
- Comic Sol's existing identity stays in the masthead and Studio; the concept's placeholder
  brand was not copied into the app. The existing page mark was recolored, not replaced.
- Animation demonstrates a physical comic. Page changes use easing and the existing deformable
  mesh. Reduced-motion preferences stop autoplay and pointer tilt; manual controls remain.
- Charcoal remains in the optional night theme and reader surround for comfortable art viewing.
  Existing console token names retain their roles to avoid a cosmetic rename across all callers.

## Artwork and replay provenance

OpenAI's built-in image generation tool created one Mena reference, four text-free panels and
a flat cover. Optimized WebP copies are local assets. One reference guided identity; the
artwork was visually inspected before use. The engine validates the storyboard, conforms
panel dimensions, adds captions, composes 1600 × 2400 pages and verifies the PDF.

The recording reaches COMPLETE at revision 17 through 18 recorded states and stores 15
actual response files. The sample's checked-in development reviews are preset fixture
decisions based on inspection, not reviews performed by a preview visitor or a vision model.
The README and DEMO panel explain this. No paid Studio provider call is needed to record it.

## Limits

Browser checks use Chromium viewport emulation, not physical phones or a screen reader.
Reduced-motion and WebGL failure paths were inspected in source; a GPU failure was not forced.
Provider integrations are covered by mocked transports, not live paid Studio keys.
The draft branch is not merged or deployed. Visual taste remains subjective.
